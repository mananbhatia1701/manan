Manan Bhatia

I am a postdoc at the mathematics department of the University of Geneva.
Previously, I was a PhD stduent at MIT Math, advisede by Scott Sheffield.
Even earlier, I was an undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore.

I work in probability theory and am specifically interested in the random geometries arising from models in the KPZ and LQG universality classes.

I can be reached at Manan.Bhatia at unige.ch.
